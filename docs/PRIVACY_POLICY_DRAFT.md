# HOMIQ Privacy Policy — DRAFT FOR PHILIPPINE LEGAL REVIEW
Effective date: To be confirmed. Personal Information Controller identity, business address, privacy contact/DPO and service-provider list: **must be supplied before publication**.

## 1. Scope and legal framework
HOMIQ processes personal data for customer, provider and administrative marketplace functions. This draft is intended to be reviewed for compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), its implementing rules and applicable National Privacy Commission guidance.

## 2. Information collected
Potential categories include name, verified contact details, account credentials (securely hashed), booking details, service address, schedule, communications, provider qualifications and verification records, service activity, quotations, wallet transaction history, support/dispute records and necessary device/security logs. Sensitive personal information, identity documents and background-check information require additional safeguards and a clearly documented lawful basis. Do not collect them in the demo without an approved process.

## 3. Purposes and lawful bases
Use data to create and secure accounts, arrange bookings, assess provider eligibility, communicate booking updates, process approved quotations and payments when enabled, prevent fraud, resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations. The applicable lawful basis must be documented for each purpose. Marketing and nonessential tracking require separate controls where consent is required.

## 4. Sharing
Share the minimum booking information needed with eligible providers; disclose full service addresses only when assignment is appropriate. Payment providers, hosting and messaging vendors may process data under contractual safeguards when integrated. Do not sell personal data. Disclose to authorities only where legally required or permitted.

## 5. Storage, security and retention
Use role-based access, secure authentication, encryption in transit, secrets management, access logging and incident response before production. Set specific retention schedules for accounts, booking records, identity checks and financial records based on documented necessity and legal obligations. Delete or anonymize data when no longer needed. Do not promise a fixed retention period before policy decisions are made.

## 6. International transfers
If vendors store or access data outside the Philippines, identify them, assess risks and implement appropriate contractual and technical protections consistent with applicable law.

## 7. Data subject rights
Users may request access, correction, erasure/blocking where applicable, object to processing, seek data portability where applicable, withdraw consent for consent-based processing and lodge complaints with the National Privacy Commission. Publish a working privacy contact and verification procedure before launch.

## 8. Children and vulnerable persons
The marketplace is intended for adults. Personal Assistance services involving vulnerable people remain gated pending enhanced screening and a separate safeguarding/privacy assessment.

## 9. Cookies and analytics
Essential storage may support sessions and security. Any optional analytics, marketing cookies or tracking SDKs must be disclosed and controlled according to applicable requirements.

## 10. Changes and contact
Publish material updates with version and effective date; obtain fresh consent where required. Provide a working privacy email, postal address and escalation process before public release.

**Publication gate:** Complete data inventory, privacy impact assessment, vendor list, retention schedule, rights workflow, breach response and Philippine counsel/DPO review.
